EXPERIMENT 1 – Standard Corruption Benchmarks (Effectiveness + Efficiency)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Show that ES-TENT reaches equal-or-better accuracy than TENT with far fewer optimisation steps on the de-facto test-time-adaptation benchmarks.

Protocol
1. Datasets   • CIFAR-10-C, CIFAR-100-C, ImageNet-C – all 15 corruption types, severities 1-5.
2. Models     • ResNet-26, ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16 (BN added to MLP blocks via "BN-adapt"), pretrained on clean data.
3. Baselines  • Source (no TTA) • TENT • ETA (entropy weight decay) • Ours (ES-TENT).
4. Stream     Online inference in batches of 64; parameters are never reset (same as TENT).
5. Metrics    • Top-1 error after every 1 k samples • Area-Under-Curve (AUC) of error vs #samples • #back-ward passes • Wall-clock time on 1×A100 • Peak GPU memory.

Expected Outcome
• For severities 3-5, ES-TENT equals or slightly surpasses TENT on final error (≈0.3-0.6 pp). 
• ES-TENT attains TENT’s final error after ≈50 % of the data (AUC ↓ 20-30 %).
• Backward passes ↓ 40-60 %; wall-clock time ↓ 30-35 %; memory identical.

What it demonstrates: The proposed scaling/skip rule accelerates convergence and reduces computation without sacrificing—and sometimes improving—robustness across a wide range of corruptions.


EXPERIMENT 2 – Ablation & Hyper-parameter Robustness (Why does it work?)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Isolate the contributions of “skip” and “scale” and test sensitivity to H_low/H_high.

Variants
A  Original TENT
B  Skip-only (TENT update skipped when H̄ < H_low)
C  Scale-only (loss = s·H̄ but never skipped)
D  Full ES-TENT.

Grid       H_low ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7}·log C;   H_high ∈ {0.8, 1.0, 1.2}·log C.
Dataset    CIFAR-10-C severity-5, 10 k samples.
Metrics    Final error, AUC, #steps, time.

Expected Outcome
• Skip-only reduces steps/time but plateaus a bit higher than TENT.
• Scale-only matches TENT speed but improves final error (~0.2 pp).
• Full ES-TENT gets the best of both worlds (lowest AUC, fewest steps).
• Performance is stable across the entire H_low/H_high grid (≤0.2 pp variance).

What it demonstrates: Both components are necessary and the method is insensitive to hyper-parameters—satisfying the “very small modification” design goal.


EXPERIMENT 3 – Continual Stream with Domain Reversals (Catastrophic Forgetting & Real-Time Savings)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Mimic a realistic scenario where the data distribution alternates between clean (in-domain) and shifted (out-of-domain) segments, and measure how well ES-TENT avoids unnecessary updates and forgetting.

Stream   30 k CIFAR-10 images arranged as:
   1-10 k  clean
 10-20 k  corruption=Gaussian noise-5
 20-25 k  back to clean
 25-30 k  corruption=JPEG 2-5 (severity increases every 1 k)

Batch     128 images, real-time budget ≤ 3 ms/img (A100).
Baselines  Source, TENT, ES-TENT.
Metrics    • Running accuracy on the current segment
            • Accuracy on a held-out clean validation set probed every 2 k imgs (measures forgetting)
            • GPU utilisation & power (nvidia-smi) averaged per segment.

Expected Outcome
• During clean segments, ES-TENT does almost zero updates, sustaining ≥ 91 % accuracy (same as source) whereas TENT slowly drifts to 89 %.
• On corrupted segments, ES-TENT adapts within ~500 images, matching TENT’s final accuracy but with 40 % fewer updates, keeping average latency < 2.5 ms/img (TENT ≈ 3.8 ms).
• After returning to clean data, ES-TENT needs no "recovery" steps and therefore shows < 0.5 pp forgetting, vs TENT’s 2-3 pp.

What it demonstrates: ES-TENT’s selective updating prevents catastrophic forgetting and delivers tangible speed/energy savings in a dynamic, real-world setting.

Taken together, the three experiments validate ES-TENT’s effectiveness from complementary angles: benchmark accuracy & efficiency (Exp 1), mechanistic insight & robustness (Exp 2), and real-time deployment benefits (Exp 3).
