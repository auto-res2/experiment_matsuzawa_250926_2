The core methodology involves training Deep Ranking Ensembles (DRE), which are ensembles of diverse neural networks, as probabilistic surrogates for HPO. DRE models the problem as a Learning-to-Rank (L2R) task, minimizing a weighted list-wise L2R loss that assigns higher penalties to top-performing hyperparameter configurations. Uncertainty is estimated by training multiple diverse neural scorers using stochastic gradient descent with different per-scorer seeds. DRE employs meta-learning by transferring knowledge from auxiliary datasets, enhancing transfer quality by conditioning scorers with dataset meta-features extracted by a DeepSet architecture. Bayesian Optimization then leverages the ensemble's mean and variance of estimated ranks with acquisition functions like Expected Improvement for guiding the search.
